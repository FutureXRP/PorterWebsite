Tank or tankless? We'll tell you the truth.

Water heaters are most of what we do, and most of the five-star reviews you'll read below. We install both kinds — and we'll recommend the one that fits your house and your budget, not the one with the bigger price tag.

Tank water heater

  • Lower upfront cost
  • Simple, proven, easy to service
  • Typical 8–12 year life
  • Often a same-week swap

Tankless water heater

  • Hot water that doesn't run out
  • Lower monthly gas or electric bill
  • 20+ year life with annual descaling
  • Frees up closet or garage space

Common questions

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Estimates are free. We look at the job, explain what it needs, and give you a written price before any work starts.

Do you do emergency or after-hours calls?

We work Monday through Saturday, 8 AM to 8 PM, which covers most evenings. We're not a 24-hour service. If you have an active leak, shut off the water at your main valve and call or text us — we'll get you on the schedule as fast as we can.

How fast can you replace a water heater?

For a standard tank, often within a day or two of your call. Tankless installs and conversions usually take a little longer to schedule because of venting and gas line work — we'll give you a realistic date up front.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Oklahoma plumbing license #00200229, verifiable through the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board at cib.ok.gov, and we carry full insurance.

What kind of warranty do you offer?

Our labor is warrantied, and installed equipment carries the manufacturer's warranty — which we register for you. If something we did isn't right, we come back and make it right.

How do I pay?

We accept all major credit and debit cards. Payment is due when the job is complete and you're satisfied.
